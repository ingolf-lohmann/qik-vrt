# Evidence boundary

The bundle separates six epistemic classes: `FORMAL_PROVED`,
`EMPIRICAL_SUPPORTED`, `SOURCE_BOUND`, `NORMATIVE`, `INTERPRETATIVE` and
`OPEN`.

A Lean kernel receipt can establish only that the exact 21 theorem declarations
type-check under Lean 4.19 and the reported imports and axiom dependencies.  It
cannot establish that:

- causal order is physically fundamental;
- the process-matrix or Quantum-Switch literature proves QIK-VRT;
- physical backward signalling or retrocausality occurs;
- Minkowski or general Lorentzian spacetime has been derived;
- the EBNF has a complete executable parser and conformance suite;
- the work has completed independent peer review or achieved field consensus;
- a normative assessment of responsibility or pride is a theorem of nature.

The primary scientific sources support only the statements and boundaries
explicitly assigned to them in the claim matrix.  Repository checks establish
software or document properties within their declared scope.  GitHub, Zenodo
and IETF receipts establish platform events and byte identities, not broader
scientific truth.
