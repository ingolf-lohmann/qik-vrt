\section*{Setup}
Let $\mathcal H_{\rm adm}$ be the admissible history space, $C:\mathcal H_{\rm adm}\to\mathcal A$ the correct continuation/recovery action, and $Obs':\mathcal H_{\rm adm}\to\mathcal O$ the available observation. Define recovery-relevant equivalence by
\[
H_1\sim_R H_2\iff C(H_1)=C(H_2).
\]
For an observation $o$, define its admissible fiber
\[
K(o)=\{H\in\mathcal H_{\rm adm}\mid Obs'(H)=o\}.
\]

\section*{Exact recovery criterion}
Deterministic recovery is justified exactly when the reachable fiber is nonempty and action-constant:
\[
\boxed{
RECOVER(o)\iff K(o)\neq\varnothing\land
\left|\{C(H)\mid H\in K(o)\}\right|=1
}.
\]
If the fiber is empty or mixes more than one required action, the safe result is fail-closed:
\[
\boxed{
K(o)=\varnothing\ \lor\
\left|\{C(H)\mid H\in K(o)\}\right|>1
\Rightarrow FAIL\_CLOSED.
}
\]
Recovery does not require reconstructing a unique past. It only requires uniqueness of the correct continuation:
\[
Recover(o)=a\iff\forall H\in K(o):C(H)=a.
\]

\section*{Kernel normal form}
The decisive information-sufficiency condition is
\[
\boxed{C=S\circ Obs_W}
\]
on the admissible history space. This is equivalent to fiber constancy:
\[
\boxed{Obs_W(H_1)=Obs_W(H_2)\Rightarrow C(H_1)=C(H_2)}.
\]
Writing kernels as equivalence relations induced by equality of outputs gives the compact condition
\[
\boxed{\ker(Obs_W)\subseteq\ker(C)}.
\]

\section*{Impossibility theorem}
If there exist admissible histories $H_1,H_2$ with
\[
Obs(H_1)=Obs(H_2),\qquad C(H_1)\neq C(H_2),
\]
then no universally correct deterministic selector $S$ can satisfy
\[
C=S\circ Obs.
\]
Proof: a deterministic $S$ receives the same observation for both histories and must return the same action, contradicting $C(H_1)\neq C(H_2)$. Hence
\[
\boxed{\ker(Obs)\not\subseteq\ker(C)\Rightarrow
\text{no universally correct deterministic recovery selector}.}
\]

\section*{Sufficiency theorem}
If $C$ is constant on every nonempty reachable observation fiber, define
\[
S(o)=\text{the unique action in }\{C(H)\mid H\in K(o)\}.
\]
Then $S$ is well-defined on $Obs'(\mathcal H_{\rm adm})$ and
\[
C=S\circ Obs'.
\]
Moreover, any two selectors satisfying this factorization agree on the reachable observation image. Outside that image they may be defined arbitrarily or fail-closed. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\ker(Obs')\subseteq\ker(C)\Rightarrow
C\text{ factors uniquely through }Obs'(\mathcal H_{\rm adm}).}
\]

\section*{Witness interpretation}
A witness is therefore neither intrinsically a third replica nor a majority voter. It is any observation refinement $Obs_W$ carrying enough information to prevent one reachable observation fiber from mixing histories that require different actions:
\[
\boxed{\ker(Obs_W)\subseteq\ker(C)}.
\]
Authority--Mirror--Witness recovery is one specialization of this general information-sufficiency theorem.

\section*{Epistemic generalization}
\[
\boxed{\textbf{Evidence is decision-sufficient exactly when its observation fibers do not mix the equivalence classes relevant to the correct action.}}
\]
This is a theorem about deterministic decision making under partial observability. It does not by itself establish physical, empirical, cryptographic, or patent claims.
